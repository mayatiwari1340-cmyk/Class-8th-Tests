The water cycle, also known as the hydrological cycle, describes the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth. Water evaporates from oceans and lakes, rises into the atmosphere, forms clouds through condensation, and falls back to the ground as precipitation. This process is essential for distributing fresh water across the planet and sustaining all forms of life.
